# ADR-0002: Car Snapshot in Bookings (No Live FK to Car Service)

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-04-30  
**Phase:** 1 — Car Service Split  

---

## Context

Phase 1 splits the `carBookingMicroservice` into two independent services:

- `carMicroservice` — owns the car catalog (`cars_db`)
- `bookingMicroservice` — owns bookings and the customer read model (`bookings_db`)

A booking must record which car was booked and at what price. In the previous monolith, `bookings.car_id` was a real PostgreSQL foreign key referencing `cars.id` in the same database. After the split, the two tables live in different databases, so a live FK across service boundaries is impossible.

## Decision

The `bookings` table stores a **car snapshot** at creation time:

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| `car_id` | Original car ID (reference, not FK) |
| `car_brand` | Car brand at booking time |
| `car_model` | Car model at booking time |
| `car_license_plate` | License plate at booking time |
| `daily_rate_snapshot_amount` | Rate used for price calculation |
| `daily_rate_snapshot_currency` | Currency of the rate |

When a booking is created, the booking service calls `GET /api/v1/cars/{id}` on the car service to fetch these details. The snapshot is written atomically with the booking row.

## Consequences

**Positive:**
- Booking history is immutable: a car can be updated, re-priced, or deleted after booking without corrupting historical records.
- This is correct DDD behavior — a booking is an aggregate that captured the state of the world at the moment it was created.
- No distributed FK means no cross-service consistency issues.

**Negative / Trade-offs:**
- The booking service has a synchronous runtime dependency on the car service at booking creation time. If the car service is down, booking creation fails. Mitigated in Phase 5 with a circuit breaker and fallback.
- Car availability checking (is this car already booked for the requested dates?) remains in the booking service's domain. The car search endpoint in the car service no longer filters by date availability — that is explicitly a booking concern.
- The `date range availability filter` was removed from `GET /api/v1/cars/search`. Clients searching for available cars for specific dates must use a future availability endpoint on the booking service (planned for Phase 5).
